FAQ

Is it free?
Yes. You can download it for free on Gumroad, Nexus Mods or GitHub. A tip on Gumroad is optional.
Which game version does it work with?
The Steam version of A Plague Tale: Innocence only.
Can I go back to the original game?
Yes: press Restore in the tool, or use Steam's Verify integrity (Library › the game › Properties › Installed Files › Verify integrity).
Do players need the tool to install a mod?
Yes. Mods made with the tool are installed with the tool.
Can I install two mods?
One Amicia mod at a time. Installing another replaces the first.
Steam updated the game.
The tool tells you at start. Press Restore; it never writes over files Steam updated.
What do I need to make a model?
Blender (tested with 5.0) and the kits the tool makes from your game. The export settings are in the tool.
How big can textures be?
Square, up to 4096 per part.

Found a problem?

Email fdxskd@gmail.com with:

  1. What you were doing: making a mod, installing, restoring, or just starting the tool. Say which step or page you were on.
  2. A screenshot of the error message, or of the whole tool window. Win + Shift + S takes one.
  3. The log files, if the problem happened while building a mod or making the kits:
    • Open the folder: in the tool, Advanced › Build log, or press Win + R, type %LOCALAPPDATA%\AmiciaModMaker\logs and press Enter.
    • Zip the whole folder and attach it.
  4. If the tool complained about your model: the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\AmiciaModMaker\inputs\checks, zipped. It holds the check report, not your model.
  5. Your setup: Windows 10 or 11, roughly where the game is installed (for example "in Program Files" or "on drive D:"), and whether Steam updated or verified the game recently.
  6. If the problem is in the game (missing textures, crash, wrong look): the chapter, a screenshot from the game, and whether pressing Restore fixes it.

Please don't send game files (.DPC), the whole tool folder, or your Blender files. They aren't needed and they're large. If your model is needed, you will be asked.
